# How to update the SFC website

A guide for Spiritwind Fellowship Church website admins. You don't need to know any code.

---

## Signing in

1. Go to the website address and add **`/admin`** at the end.
   **https://spiritwindfc.github.io/spiritwindfc-web/admin/**
2. Click **Sign In with GitHub** and log in with your GitHub account.
   (The first time, GitHub asks you to **Authorize** the website admin. Click the green button.)
3. You'll see a menu on the left: **Events, Sermons, Photo gallery, Ministries, School of Bereans, Home page, Church info & contacts.**

> Can't sign in? Ask the web developer to check that your GitHub account was added to the church's organization.

---

## The golden rule: Save, then wait a minute

When you finish editing, click **Save** (top right). Your change is published automatically. **The live website updates in about 1–2 minutes.** If you don't see it yet, wait a moment and refresh the page.

Every save is recorded, so **mistakes can always be undone**. Ask the developer if you need an older version back.

## Common tasks

### Add an upcoming event
1. **Events → Upcoming events**.
2. Click **Add Event** (it appears at the top).
3. Fill in the **Event name** and **Date**. Time, place, description, photo and link are optional.
4. **Save**.

- The soonest event appears in the big orange card on the website.
- **Past events disappear by themselves** the day after. You don't have to delete them (but you can, to keep the list tidy).
- Don't add the regular Sunday Celebration. It already shows automatically.

### Add a sermon
1. **Sermons**.
2. Click **Add Sermon** (it appears at the top).
3. Add the **title or key quote**, a **thumbnail** (the quote graphic you posted on Facebook works well), and the **video link** from Facebook or YouTube. Speaker, Scripture and date are optional but nice.
4. To show it in the large card, switch **Featured** on, and switch it **off** on the old featured sermon.
5. **Save**.

### Change photos
- **Home page → Top banner → Background photo**: the big photo at the very top. Wide (landscape) photos work best.
- **Home page → Welcome section → Photo 1 / 2 / 3**: the three photos beside the welcome text. Please fill in **Describe the photo** (helps blind visitors and Google).
- **Photo gallery**: add, remove or **drag to reorder** photos. Choose *Tall* for portrait photos and *Wide* for landscape ones. Nine photos fill the grid nicely.

When you pick a photo you can **upload a new one** or **choose one already on the site**. Uploaded photos are automatically resized and compressed, so phone photos are fine.

**Please only use photos the church took or has permission to use.**

### Update service times, address or contacts
**Church info & contacts**:
- **Service times**: the *first* service is the main one shown at the top of the website. Add more for midweek services.
- **Address**, **Google Maps link**, **Email**, **Phone** (leave empty to hide it).
- **Social media links**.

### School of Bereans
**School of Bereans**: update the introduction, **class schedule**, **enrollment link** (e.g. a Google Form), the poster, the latest ceremony, and **what each level covers** (Lamb → Sheep → Dove → Eagle → Lion).

### Ministries
**Ministries**: add a card for each ministry with a name, photo, short description and when it meets.

### About us, Bible verses, Plan your visit
**Home page**:
- **About us cards**: Vision, Mission and What We Believe. Leave *Short statement* empty to show "Coming soon".
- **Scripture carousel**: the verses that rotate in the orange section.
- **Plan your visit**: first-visit steps, kids & families, and getting there / parking.

---

## Tips

- **Keep it short.** Visitors skim. One or two sentences per item is plenty.
- **Check on your phone** after saving. Most visitors use phones.
- A field marked with a red message must be filled in before you can save.
- **Don't change** *Church info & contacts → Contact form* unless the web developer asks you to.
- If something looks broken after a save, tell the developer. Nothing is ever lost.

Privacy

Privacy policy.

Draft — for the church to review and approve

Spiritwind Fellowship Church ("SFC", "we") respects your privacy. This page explains what we collect through this website and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Information you choose to send through the contact form: your name, email address, the topic you pick and your message.
  • Basic technical data your browser sends to any website, such as your browser type, which our web host may log.

How we use it

We use your message only to reply to you, to pray for any requests you share, and to help you connect with the church. We do not sell or rent your information.

Who can see it

Messages are seen by church staff and volunteers who respond to inquiries. Our form provider delivers them to the church's email.

Your choices

You can ask us to view, correct or delete the information you sent by emailing spiritwindfellowshipchurch@gmail.com. We handle personal data in line with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).

Third-party sites

This site links to Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and Google Maps. Those services have their own privacy policies.
